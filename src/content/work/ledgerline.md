Ledgerline had a loyal audience and a product that quietly worked against them.
Every screen asked for attention; nothing helped people feel in control. We were
brought in to rebuild trust — starting with the brand and ending with a shipped app.

## The problem

Growth had layered feature on feature until the interface felt like a spreadsheet
wearing a costume. New users churned within a week; power users tolerated it.

## What we did

We restructured the information architecture around a single question — *"am I okay?"* —
and let everything else recede. A restrained identity system, a calmer palette, and a
motion language that rewards rather than distracts. Then our team built the iOS and
Android apps against a shared design system so the two never drift.

## Outcome

The redesign paid for itself inside a quarter: engagement up, ratings up, and a
support queue that finally had room to breathe.
