Atlas ran buses, bikes and scooters under three brands that behaved like strangers.
Riders were confused; the operator was invisible. We unified everything into one
identity that works on a screen, a station, and the side of a vehicle.

## The problem

Fragmented branding made a single service feel like three unreliable ones. Wayfinding
was inconsistent and the apps didn't talk to each other.

## What we did

We designed a flexible identity and wayfinding system built for motion and small screens,
then built the native rider app and the operator web platform on top of it. One account,
one look, every mode of transport.

## Outcome

A million-plus riders, more completed trips, and a brand that scales city by city.
