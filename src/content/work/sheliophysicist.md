ICF helps entrepreneurs and MSMEs across India set up factories — detailed
project reports, government subsidy and loan assistance, machinery sourcing,
and turnkey factory setup. Fifteen years of that work had never been given a
front door that matched it.

## The problem

Manufacturing consultancy is a trust business, bought largely on reputation and
referral. ICF's old presence undersold the track record — a serious firm that
looked like a directory listing.

## What we did

A restrained industrial identity built around a navy-and-blue palette and a
plain-spoken wordmark, then a website that leads with proof: sectors served,
capital deployed, banks on the panel. Service pages are structured so a founder
can find the exact help they need — DPR, subsidy, machinery, setup — in one step.

## Outcome

A site that carries the firm's authority on first read, and a system the ICF
team can extend to every sector and city page without losing the thread.
