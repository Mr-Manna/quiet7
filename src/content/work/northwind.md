Northwind sold beautifully made gear through a brand that had stopped evolving in 2009.
The equity was real; the expression was tired. We refreshed the identity without
erasing its history, then rebuilt the storefront on a modern, headless stack.

## The problem

The old site was slow, hard to update, and looked nothing like the products it sold.
Younger customers bounced before the hero image loaded.

## What we did

We kept the wordmark's bones and rebuilt everything around it — a warmer palette, a
confident editorial grid, and photography direction that let the product lead. The new
store runs on a headless commerce backend so the team ships campaigns in hours, not weeks.

## Outcome

Faster, warmer, and measurably more memorable — with a revenue line to prove it.
