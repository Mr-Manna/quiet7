Cadence was growing faster than its interface could keep up with. Each squad shipped
its own patterns; clinicians paid the tax. We built the system that let them move fast
without breaking trust — then a marketing site to match the ambition.

## The problem

Inconsistent UI slowed every release and eroded confidence in a product where confidence
is everything. There was no shared language between design and engineering.

## What we did

A token-driven design system, documented and coded, became the single source of truth.
Accessibility was a requirement, not an afterthought — every component ships AA-compliant.
The new marketing site turns a complex platform into a clear, credible story.

## Outcome

Teams ship three times faster, trials convert better, and the whole product finally
speaks with one voice.
