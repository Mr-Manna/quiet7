Before a visitor reads a word, they've already felt your colour. It arrives faster than
language, straight past the rational mind, and sets the tone for everything that follows.

## One colour, said well

Brands often reach for a rainbow when a single, committed hue would do more. A restrained
palette — one confident accent against a disciplined neutral ground — is easier to remember
and harder to imitate. It becomes a signature.

Think of the brands you can picture with your eyes closed. Almost all of them own a colour.
Not a palette — *a colour.*

## Context is everything

The same green reads as growth in one setting and toxicity in another. Colour has no fixed
meaning; it borrows it from culture, from contrast, from what sits beside it. That's why we
never choose colour in isolation — only in relationship.

Used well, it stops being decoration and becomes a way of speaking.
